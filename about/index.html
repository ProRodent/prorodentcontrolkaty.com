One number covers everything.

Booking, questions and urgent calls all go through (888) 686-4821. The person who answers will ask three things: where the trouble is, what you noticed and how long it has been going on. From those answers they offer the soonest opening that fits.

Nights, weekends and holidays use the same number, and nobody asks you to leave a message and wait.

What a typical job includes.

  • A walk-through of the attic, garage, crawl space, yard and roofline.
  • Trapping or station placement matched to the animal found.
  • Sealing with sheet metal, steel mesh and hard-setting sealant.
  • A return trip to count catches, test the seals and arrange cleanup or repair.

The sequence is fixed because each step protects the next. Closing a gap while an animal is still behind it can trap it in the wall, and scrubbing an attic before the entries are shut means scrubbing it again.

Claims this site leaves out.

You will not find a founding year, license number, staff biography, award or customer quote here. None of those were supplied to us in a form we could stand behind, and an invented one would be worse than none. Ask the technician for credentials at the door, as you would with any trade.

We also make no promise that rodents will never return. Fruit trees, fences, drainage ditches and neighboring lots keep animals nearby. What we promise is a thorough search, durable materials and a check afterward.

Poison, cleanup and health.

Rodenticides are regulated by the EPA, whose page is linked under sources. We prefer traps and sealing, and if a product is ever placed, we tell you which one and where.

Cleanup follows the CDC routine of airing out, soaking, wiping and bagging. Pest work is our field. For anything about your own health, a doctor is the right person to ask.
