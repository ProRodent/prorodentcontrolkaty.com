Safety checklist for rodent droppings

Read through every line before handling anything, because a short pause spares your lungs.

  • Hurried cleaning makes dust, so go slowly and leave the brush alone.
  • Prop windows and doors open, stay outdoors for about thirty minutes, and let the air turn over.
  • Start with gloves on and end with a hand wash.
  • Leave droppings alone until they are drenched and have sat for around five minutes.
  • Bag, knot, bin. Use protection whenever a carcass is handled.
  • Flu-type symptoms after a cleanup should be mentioned to a physician, since diagnosing them is not our work.
