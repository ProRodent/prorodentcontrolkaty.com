How the services build on each other.

Five stages make up a whole job: look, trap, seal, clean, repair. Cleanup is held until late, because scrubbing an attic that still has visitors only creates more work.

Plenty of customers need only one stage. Someone who plugged every hole alone might need cleanup only, and a shop manager might need regular checks and nothing else. Tell us what is done, and we plan around what remains.

Urgent calls do not get a separate tier, since the work stays identical. Someone answers all night, and you can request a same-day slot from any page on this site when the schedule has room.

Service questions, answered.

Where should I start reading?

When you cannot say what is living in your house, open the inspection page. The visit names the animal, finds every entry and delivers a plan, all before you spend on bigger work.

May I book sealing only, or cleanup only?

Yes. Describe what has been done already and the visit is built around what is left over.

Are cars and yards covered too?

Yes. Vehicles have a page about engine bays and cabin air intakes, and yards have a page about burrows, sheds, shrubbery and feeders.

Which page fits a shop or an office?

Business owners should read the commercial page, which covers recurring checks, treatment and the written report left after each stop.
