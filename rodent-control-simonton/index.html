Before you call: handling droppings safely

Everything here repeats guidance from public health bodies, so any household can use it whether or not it ever books a service.

  • Aged pellets turn to powder that people breathe in, so leave them unswept.
  • Unseen hazards can linger in stale air, so open the space, step away and give it half an hour.
  • Shield your hands at the start and scrub them at the end.
  • Soak, wait, wipe: that is the sequence, with a five-minute pause in the middle.
  • Tie the bag tightly before carrying it out, and wear a barrier on your hands whenever a dead rodent must be shifted.
  • Fever, aches or breathlessness following contact with waste belong with a clinic rather than an exterminator.
