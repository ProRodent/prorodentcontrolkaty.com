If your home dates from that era.

We cannot say which homes in Kelliwood were built when, and many may have been updated. If yours is from the 1970s or 1980s, a few components are worth a look, because they wear with time. Garage door seals flatten, vent screens tear, soffit panels loosen and attic insulation settles.

Trees, roofs and the routes between them.

A tree that has grown for decades can spread limbs across a roof. A roof rat can stroll along a branch to a soffit and squeeze through a gap that seems far too small. Trimming limbs back from the roof removes a bridge, and the work is best done by a tree service.

We check where limbs meet the roof, then examine the soffit, fascia and vents beneath. Where a gap is found, exclusion work closes it with metal and mesh.

Garages, sheds and storage.

Garages in a development like this commonly hold years of stored items. Cardboard, holiday boxes and old furniture are nest material, and shelving against a wall hides the base of the wall. Moving items onto shelves and into sealed tubs helps more than most people expect.

  • Lift boxes off the floor and a few inches from the wall.
  • Replace cardboard with plastic tubs for long storage.
  • Check the door seal and the corners for light.
  • Look in sheds and outbuildings for droppings and nests.

Our garage page goes into the details.

Hot weather, storms and attics.

Attics in this climate get very hot, and animals nest where it is shaded or insulated. After storms, check for torn screens, loose flashing and debris at the foundation. Noises that start at dusk may come from the attic or the wall by the garage.

Phones are answered at any hour. A same-day visit is possible when the schedule has room, and we tell you the first available window on the call.

What to tell the dispatcher.

Give your street address, say whether you have a slab or a raised floor, and describe any trees that touch the roof. Mention a shed, a shared fence or a pet that roams. With those details, we arrive prepared.

Handy to mention.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Trees whose limbs touch the roof.
  • Condition of the garage door seal.
  • Attic noise or smell, and when it began.

Do these ahead of time.

  • Decant pet kibble and wild-bird seed into lidded tubs tonight.
  • Lift water bowls before bedtime and wipe down the counters.
  • Resist setting your own traps for now, because that scrambles the activity pattern.
  • Record the day the first noise or odor appeared.
