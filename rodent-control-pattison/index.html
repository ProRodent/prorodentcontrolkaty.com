Fruit trees, pet food and feeders.

Food is what keeps rodents returning. Fallen fruit, a bag of seed with a loose lid and a bowl left out overnight are easy to fix. Picking up fruit every day or two and storing food in lidded tubs often lowers activity before a trap is set.

We still need to close the entries. A food fix without sealing leaves the door open, and sealing without a food fix leaves the invitation. The yard page and the exclusion page describe how the two fit together.
