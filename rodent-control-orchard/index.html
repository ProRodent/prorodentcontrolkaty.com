Feed, seed and animal bins.

If you keep animals or store seed, the bins matter. A metal or hard plastic bin with a tight lid denies rodents a meal, and a bag left open on a shelf does the opposite. Tell us what you store and where.

Also tell us about any chicken coop, kennel or shed. Animals often nest near a feed source before moving to the house, so we look there during the walk-through.
